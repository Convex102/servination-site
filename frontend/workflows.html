Co-ordinating multiple assistants

The workflow engine lets you chain multiple assistants so the output of one becomes the context for the next. This mirrors how a real team would collaborate across finance, risk and compliance, but with AI doing the heavy lifting.

  • Define a sequence of steps, each using a different assistant.
  • Pass key outputs forward as structured context.
  • Capture the full trail for auditability and review.
  • Standardise recurring reviews as reusable workflows.

Example workflow

  1. FinRes reviews a quarterly P&L and cashflow.
  2. QCRisk identifies operational and control risks.
  3. RegWatch highlights regulatory exposure and board-level issues.

Workflow demo: board pack preparation

A typical workflow demo for a board or risk committee pack might look like this:

  1. Upload the latest management accounts and KPI pack.
  2. Run a “Board pack review” workflow preconfigured in the client area.
  3. Review the consolidated summary, risk commentary and regulatory points.
  4. Export the narrative into your board paper template.

This gives a consistent, repeatable review process for each reporting cycle.
